# Competitor Verification: Zinnia

- **Checked date:** 2026-11-25
- **Official sources:**
  - `pixiteapps.com/apps/zinnia-digital-journaling-app/` and Apple App Store listing (verified via live search 2026-11-25)

## Verified Facts (2026-11-25)
- **Pricing:**
  - **Free tier:** Limited preview with restricted number of journals and pages.
  - **Subscription:** ~1-week free trial, then approximately $9.99/month or $34.99-$39.99/year (varies by region; supports Apple Family Sharing; no permanent free core canvas without page caps).
- **Platforms:** iPhone and iPad (heavily optimized for iPad + Apple Pencil) with iCloud sync.
- **Strengths:**
  - Realistic drawing, calligraphy, brush, and handwriting tools built for Apple Pencil.
  - Huge Zinnia Studio asset library (pre-made planners, habit/mood trackers, washi tape, journal cards) updated monthly.
  - Calendar sync (e.g., Google Calendar), productivity templates (Kanban, timeblocking), and internal page hyperlinking.
- **Structural trade-offs (switching triggers toward PinyPiny):**
  - Designed primarily for Apple Pencil drawing and structured bullet-journal planning; can feel heavy if you just want to arrange a few iPhone photos and short captions with your thumb.
  - Free tier restricts journal/page counts and pushes a recurring subscription (~$35-$40/year) for full use, with no one-time Lifetime Pro option.
  - Relies heavily on curated Studio sticker packs rather than turning objects from your own photos into on-device cutout stickers in seconds.
