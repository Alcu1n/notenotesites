# Competitor Verification: Apple Journal

- **Checked date:** 2026-11-25
- **Official sources:**
  - Apple Journal official feature pages (`apple.com`)
  - Apple App Store listing & iOS/iPadOS/macOS documentation (verified via live search 2026-11-25)

## Verified Facts (2026-11-25)
- **Pricing:** 100% free ($0), built into iOS/iPadOS/macOS with end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync included at no extra charge.
- **Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, and Mac (expanded from iPhone-only to iPad and Mac).
- **Strengths:**
  - Automatic on-device Journaling Suggestions pulling recent photos, outings, workouts, locations, and music.
  - Supports photos, videos, audio recordings, State of Mind emotion logging, Map view, multiple journals, and Face ID/Touch ID lock.
  - Apple Pencil handwriting and sketching on iPad; native keyboard writing on Mac.
- **Structural trade-offs (switching triggers toward PinyPiny):**
  - Linear, chronological feed layout rather than a freeform spatial collage canvas (cannot freely tilt, layer, or arrange floating photos, rotated text, and custom stickers on textured paper).
  - No built-in Sticker Studio to cut out subjects from your own photos into decorative journal stickers.
  - Entries live in a vertical list rather than a tactile paper card stack built for visual image export as shareable postcard-style pages.
