# Competitor Verification: Day One

- **Checked date:** 2026-11-25
- **Official sources:**
  - `dayoneapp.com` pricing and plan comparison (verified via live search 2026-11-25)

## Verified Facts (2026-11-25)
- **Pricing:**
  - **Basic (Free):** $0, unlimited text entries and unlimited journals, end-to-end encryption, local only (no cross-device sync), capped at **1 photo per entry**, no video/audio.
  - **Silver (formerly Premium):** $49.99 USD/year (billed annually; 1-month trial for new users; no monthly or one-time lifetime option). Adds cross-device sync (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Web), up to 30 media items per entry, audio transcription, IFTTT/Zapier/Strava integrations, 25% off book printing.
  - **Gold:** $74.99 USD/year. Adds AI reflection features (Daily Chat, highlights, AI image generation) and 35% off book printing.
- **Strengths:**
  - Cross-platform support across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and Web.
  - Deep long-form Markdown writing, audio transcription, automated integrations, and integrated physical book printing.
  - Mature multi-year archive tools ("On This Day", tags, streaks, end-to-end encryption).
- **Structural trade-offs (switching triggers toward PinyPiny):**
  - Free tier limits each entry to 1 photo; multi-photo layouts and sync require a $49.99/year recurring subscription with no lifetime purchase option.
  - Text-first chronological stream rather than a freeform spatial scrapbook canvas.
  - No on-device photo-to-sticker cutout studio or visual paper card-stack browsing.
  - Account-centric cloud architecture for synced features vs PinyPiny's no-account local-first design with optional private Apple iCloud sync.
