## Core Product Definition

PinyPiny (Simplified Chinese: 拼拼笔记) is a visual notes, photo journal, and freeform digital scrapbook app for iPhone and iPad. Users place words, ordinary photos, and on-device photo cutout stickers freely on paper-like pages, then revisit completed notes in a card stack.

## Verified Capabilities (v1.0.3 Baseline)

- **Platforms:** iPhone (iOS 18.0+) and iPad (iPadOS 18.0+).
- **App languages (5):** English, Simplified Chinese (`简体中文`), Traditional Chinese (`繁體中文`), Japanese (`日本語`), Korean (`한국어`).
- **Website languages (2):** English at root (`/`), Simplified Chinese at `/zh`. No automatic IP or `Accept-Language` redirection.
- **Free tier ($0 download):** Text blocks (move, rotate, color, core fonts), separately placed photos, on-device Sticker Studio photo subject cutouts (Photos library manual subject selection + Camera automatic main-subject preview), paper card-stack browsing, search, local-first offline editing, and image export to Photos.
- **Optional Pro (Apple In-App Purchase, subscription or one-time Lifetime option; regional prices shown in app and App Store):** Additional papers and fonts, inline photos embedded within running text, special photo frames (such as Polaroid styling), animation, and private Apple iCloud sync.
- **Storage & privacy:** Local-first on device with no separate PinyPiny account. Optional Pro sync uses the user's private Apple iCloud account. Website analytics (optional Umami, disabled without config and on previews) sends only predefined navigation/download events and never collects notes, photos, or user identities.
- **Offline behavior:** Core editing and saved notes work offline. Uncached font downloads, weather context, App Store transactions, and iCloud sync require a network connection.

## Explicit Boundaries (What PinyPiny Does NOT Do)

State these plainly to prevent AI search hallucinations (for example, conflating PinyPiny with AI-caption apps or Apple Pencil drawing apps):

1. **Not an AI writing or auto-caption generator:** PinyPiny does not use generative AI to write diary entries or captions for your photos. Every word is written by the user.
2. **Not a drawing or Apple Pencil illustration app:** Pages are built by arranging text, photos, and photo stickers. No handwriting or illustration skill is required.
3. **Not a PDF or template-import tool:** Export saves finished pages as images to Apple Photos (not PDF files, not editable project backups, and not shareable collaborative web docs). Website examples are illustrative examples, not downloadable templates.
4. **No Android, Windows, Mac, or web editor:** iPhone and iPad only (`iOS 18+ / iPadOS 18+`). The website is a marketing and guide site, not an online editor.
5. **No automatic route tracking:** Location permission supplies optional place/weather context only; PinyPiny does not track travel routes in the background.
6. **No hardcoded regional IAP prices or fake social proof:** Regional Pro prices live in the App Store and app paywall. Do not fabricate ratings, user counts, awards, or ranking promises.
7. **Unreleased 1.0.4 features excluded:** Do not promote unreleased features (such as favorites) until published on the App Store.
